## What this is

A free, gentle companion for your day: one card of affirmation or reflection for each day of the year. Every card was written for Setanta Hypnotherapy. They support calm, self-compassion, resilience and steady personal growth between sessions, or whenever you need a moment to pause.

There's nothing to install and no account to create. It works on your phone, tablet or computer.

## How to use it

Open the link above and use the buttons under the card:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ☀ **Today's Card** | Shows the card for today's date (1 January = Card 1, 31 December = Card 365) |
| ✨ **Random Card** | Picks a card at random, for when you want a message that finds you |
| ◀ **Previous** / **Next** ▶ | Moves through the deck in order |

**Keyboard shortcuts** (on a computer): `←` / `→` previous and next · `T` today's card · `R` or `Space` random card.

### Handy links to save or bookmark

- **Start with today's card:**
  https://goyaklaarchimedes.github.io/setanta-deck/?mode=day
- **Start with a random card:**
  https://goyaklaarchimedes.github.io/setanta-deck/?mode=random

**Tip:** On your phone, open the "today's card" link and choose **Add to Home Screen**. You'll then have the deck one tap away each morning.

## A suggested daily practice

1. Find a quiet moment, perhaps first thing in the morning or before sleep.
2. Take three slow breaths before you read the card.
3. Read it once silently, then once aloud if you can.
4. Notice where it lands in your body, and carry one word or phrase with you through the day.

## Please note

These cards support your wellbeing. They don't replace professional, medical or psychological care. If you are struggling, please contact your therapist, your GP or a local support service.
