A gentle daily practice

  1. Find a quiet moment, perhaps first thing in the morning or before sleep.
  2. Take three slow breaths before you read the card.
  3. Read it once silently, then once aloud if you can.
  4. Notice where it lands in your body, and carry one word or phrase with you through the day.
